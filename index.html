Professional Focus

I am a digital systems developer and memory worker whose practice centers on building structured, sustainable archive systems for institutions and community-oriented projects. My work emphasizes organizing, preserving, and providing long-term access to records such as staff data, administrative documentation, and historical materials that contribute to institutional memory.

I approach development not only as a technical task, but as a form of stewardship—one that requires care, ethical decision-making, and cultural awareness, particularly when working with records connected to Black institutions and communities.

Approach to Archiving & Memory Work

My practice is grounded in the belief that digital tools and web-based systems play a critical role in preserving Black institutional history and digital presence. I am especially interested in how databases, web applications, and archiving workflows can be aligned with culturally informed practices, community needs, and responsible preservation standards.

As my work expands beyond internal record systems, I am intentionally engaging with web archiving as a way to capture, preserve, and contextualize Black digital life as it appears online—ensuring it is treated as historically valuable rather than temporary or disposable.
